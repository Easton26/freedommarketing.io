Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Freedom Marketing ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through freedommarketing.io, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, such as when you fill out our contact form, book a call or email us. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number and business name
  • Details about your business and advertising goals that you share in your message

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We use this only to keep the site running securely.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and schedule strategy calls
  • To provide, manage and report on the services you hire us for
  • To send you information related to your enquiry or account
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Client Advertising Accounts

If you become a client, we may be given access to your Meta Business Manager, ad accounts, pixel data and related reporting. We use this access only to deliver the services you've hired us for, and we handle that data according to Meta's platform terms and our agreement with you.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to run our website and business. They only receive the information needed to perform their service:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and delivers them to our inbox
  • Vercel — hosts this website
  • Google Fonts — provides the fonts used on this site
  • Email and scheduling tools — used to communicate with you and book calls

Some of these providers may store or process information outside of Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

5. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party services listed above may set technical cookies needed for them to work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. When it's no longer needed, we delete it securely.

7. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections and access limited to people who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take protecting your information seriously.

8. Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to us using it. To make a request, email us at the address below and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at easton@freedommarketing.io.
